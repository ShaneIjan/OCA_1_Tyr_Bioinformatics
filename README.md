## Part D. Genome Browser Tracks

**a. Gene annotation track used:**  
The NCBI RefSeq gene annotation track was used to examine the *TYR* gene.

**b. ClinVar-related variant marks:**  
Yes. Numerous ClinVar-related variant marks were visible within and near the TYR genomic region when the ClinVar Variants track was turned on.

**c. Conservation of regions:**  
Yes. The 100 Vertebrates Conservation by PhyloP track showed different levels of conservation across the TYR region, with some regions displaying stronger conservation peaks than others.

**d. Location of conserved regions:**  
The stronger conservation signals appeared mainly around some exonic regions, although conservation was also observed in some intronic or other non-coding regions.
